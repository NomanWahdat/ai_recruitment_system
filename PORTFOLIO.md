# Portfolio evidence

This existing Django/Next.js project was improved incrementally after a source-code audit.

The contribution is ownership-aware authorization, private documents, persistent processing, evidence correction, explicit human decisions, measured evaluation and automated verification.

Review the [README](README.md), [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), [design decisions](docs/DECISIONS.md), [evaluation](docs/EVALUATION.md), [verification](docs/VERIFICATION.md), and [security model](docs/SECURITY.md).

A seeded score, generated explanation or screenshot is not evidence of hiring accuracy. The reports distinguish regression checks, developer-authored relevance grades, browser behavior, and unmeasured live-provider quality.
