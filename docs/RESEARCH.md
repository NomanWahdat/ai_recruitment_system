# Research opportunities

1. Explainable matching versus simple baselines: compare exact skills, TF-IDF, semantic retrieval and the rubric using independently annotated pairs, NDCG/Recall@k and explanation evidence precision.
2. Robustness: measure formatting, missing sections, aliases, keyword stuffing and embedded instructions. Separate local parser behavior from live-model injection resistance.
3. Privacy-aware evaluation: compare utility and information exposure before/after identity removal and profile minimization.

Use consented/de-identified data, independent annotation, a held-out split and explicit limitations. These are proposals, not completed research or scholarship-admission guarantees.
